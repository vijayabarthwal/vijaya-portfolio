Project Case Study

Luxury Fashion Campaign

An editorial fashion campaign created in Canva, exploring luxury aesthetics, typography, visual hierarchy, imagery, and composition.

Objective

The goal was to create a premium fashion campaign that communicates a modern luxury identity through strong imagery, typography, spacing, and visual composition.

Creative Direction

I chose a clean editorial direction with restrained styling, strong typography, and a high-fashion visual approach.

The composition was designed to create a clear visual hierarchy between the campaign title, seasonal information, and imagery.

Design Process

I combined the campaign title, seasonal text, fashion photography, spacing, and supporting elements into a balanced editorial layout.

I refined the placement and proportions of each element to maintain a sophisticated and cohesive presentation.

Tools & Skills

Canva Graphic Design Editorial Design Typography Visual Hierarchy Composition

Final Design

The completed campaign combines editorial typography, fashion imagery, and a minimal luxury aesthetic into one cohesive visual.

What I Learned

This project strengthened my understanding of how typography, spacing, imagery, and visual hierarchy work together to create stronger designs.

It also gave me practical experience creating an editorial campaign layout in Canva with attention to consistency and presentation.

Outcome

The final campaign demonstrates my developing skills in graphic design, editorial composition, typography, and visual storytelling.
