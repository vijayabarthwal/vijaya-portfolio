Project Case Study

Luxury Fashion Campaign

An editorial fashion campaign created in Canva, exploring luxury aesthetics, typography, visual hierarchy, imagery, and sophisticated composition.

🎯 Objective

The objective was to create a premium-looking fashion campaign that communicates a modern luxury identity through strong imagery, typography, spacing, and visual composition.

🎨 Creative Direction

I chose a clean editorial direction with a restrained color palette, strong typography, and a high-fashion visual style.

The design focuses on creating visual hierarchy so that the campaign title, seasonal information, and fashion imagery work together naturally.

🛠️ Design Process

I developed the composition by combining the campaign title, seasonal text, fashion photography, spacing, and supporting visual elements.

I refined the placement and proportions of the elements to create a balanced editorial layout and maintain a sophisticated overall appearance.

💻 Tools & Skills

Canva Graphic Design Editorial Design Typography Visual Hierarchy Composition

✨ Final Design

The completed campaign combines editorial typography, fashion imagery, and a minimal luxury aesthetic into one cohesive visual.

📚 What I Learned

This project helped me understand how typography, spacing, imagery, and visual hierarchy can work together to create a stronger design.

I also gained practical experience creating an editorial campaign layout in Canva while paying attention to consistency and presentation.

🚀 Outcome

The final result is a fashion campaign concept that demonstrates my developing skills in graphic design, editorial composition, typography, and visual storytelling.
