LinkedIn · Professional 02

Learning is a habit.

Not another item on your to-do list.

Professional Post

What if learning didn't require a free afternoon?

We often wait for the “right time” to learn something new. When the schedule is lighter. When work slows down. When we finally have enough time. But learning can start much smaller. Twenty focused minutes can be enough to explore a tool, understand a concept, or practice a skill. The goal isn't to finish everything today. The goal is to keep moving.
