Motivational Short

A short-form motivational video combining visual storytelling, creative writing, pacing, and video editing.

Project Overview

This personal project tells a simple motivational journey through three visual moments: sunrise, driving, and a quiet forest landscape. Each scene represents a different stage of moving forward.

Creative Approach

  • Sunrise: A new beginning.
  • Driving: Keep moving forward.
  • Forest: Find and follow your own path.

Process

  • Selected suitable visual clips.
  • Created concise motivational text.
  • Matched text and visuals to create a natural flow.
  • Edited and reviewed the final short-form video.
