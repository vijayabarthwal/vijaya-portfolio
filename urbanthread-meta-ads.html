04 · Audience

Targeting & Audience Strategy

Primary Audience

Young adults aged 18–30 in India who are interested in streetwear, casual fashion, and online shopping.

Audience Characteristics

  • Interested in modern and trendy clothing
  • Follows fashion and streetwear content
  • Comfortable shopping online
  • Looks for affordable and expressive everyday fashion
  • Active on Instagram and Facebook

Interest Targeting

  • Streetwear
  • Casual Fashion
  • Fashion & Clothing
  • Online Shopping
  • T-Shirts
  • Urban Fashion

Targeting Approach

The campaign focuses on a younger, fashion-conscious audience that is likely to engage with visually appealing streetwear content and make online clothing purchases.

Creative Strategy

The oversized T-shirt creative uses a bold visual identity and a short, memorable tagline to capture attention while scrolling. The messaging focuses on individuality, confidence, and everyday style.

Expected Customer Journey

Ad Impression → Engagement → Website Visit → Product Interest → Add to Cart → Purchase
05 · Optimization

Campaign Optimization Strategy

01

Monitor CTR

Track the Click-Through Rate to evaluate how effectively the creative and messaging attract attention.

02

Monitor CPC

Keep track of Cost Per Click and identify opportunities to reduce the cost of driving website traffic.

03

Evaluate Conversions

Compare Add-to-Cart and Purchase data to understand how effectively website visitors are converting into customers.

04

Creative Testing

Test different visuals, taglines, headlines, and primary text to identify the combinations that generate the strongest engagement and conversions.

05

Audience Testing

Compare different interest groups and audience segments to identify which audience produces the best results.

06

Budget Optimization

If one audience or creative performs significantly better, gradually shift more of the available budget toward the stronger-performing combination.

07

Retargeting

Create a retargeting audience for people who interacted with the advertisement or visited the website but did not complete a purchase.

Optimization Goal

Improve engagement, reduce acquisition costs, increase conversions, and maximize Return on Ad Spend (ROAS).

06 · Learnings

Campaign Learnings & Key Takeaways

1. Strong Visuals Matter

A bold and simple creative can help capture attention in a highly competitive social media environment.

2. Audience Relevance is Important

Targeting people whose interests and shopping behavior match the product can improve the quality of traffic and engagement.

3. Clear Messaging Improves Action

A concise primary message combined with a strong headline and clear CTA can guide users toward the desired action.

4. Performance Data Drives Decisions

Metrics such as CTR, CPC, Add-to-Cart, Purchases, and ROAS help identify what is working and where improvements are needed.

5. Testing Improves Campaign Performance

Testing different creatives, audiences, and messaging can help identify the combinations that deliver stronger results.

6. Retargeting Can Recover Lost Conversions

Users who interact with an advertisement but do not purchase can be reached again through retargeting campaigns.

07 · Conclusion

Key Takeaway

This campaign demonstrates how a structured Meta Ads strategy can combine audience research, creative development, persuasive ad copy, performance measurement, and continuous optimization to support an e-commerce sales objective.
