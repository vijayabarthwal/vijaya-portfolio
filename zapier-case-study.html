Customer Inquiry Email Tracker

An automated Gmail-to-Google Sheets workflow built with Zapier to capture, organize, and track customer inquiry emails.

Project Overview

The goal was to reduce repetitive manual work when collecting customer inquiries from email.

The workflow connects Gmail → Zapier → Google Sheets to automatically organize relevant email information into a structured tracking system.

The Problem

Customer inquiries can become difficult to track when they are mixed with other emails. Manually copying information into a spreadsheet also takes time and can lead to missed or inconsistent records.

How the Workflow Works

01

Gmail Trigger

Identifies incoming messages that match the configured criteria.

02

Zapier

Processes the matching email and passes the relevant information to the next step.

03

Google Sheets

Stores the information in a structured tracking sheet for review.

Information Captured

Date Sender Subject Email Body Email ID

Tools & Skills

Gmail Zapier Google Sheets Workflow Design Automation

Testing & Problem Solving

I tested the workflow with multiple emails and reviewed the resulting records in Google Sheets to verify that the information was being captured and organized correctly.

This project helped me develop a practical workflow of build → test → identify → fix → verify.

Outcome

The project demonstrates how everyday email-based tasks can be turned into a more organized and repeatable workflow using no-code automation tools.
