Customer Inquiry Email Tracker

An automated Gmail-to-Google Sheets workflow designed to capture, organize, and track customer inquiry emails using Zapier.

Gmail Zapier Google Sheets Workflow Automation

Project Overview

The goal of this project was to create a simple automation that could reduce the repetitive work involved in collecting customer inquiry emails.

Instead of manually copying information from incoming emails into a spreadsheet, the workflow connects Gmail → Zapier → Google Sheets so relevant messages can be organized into a structured tracking system.

The Problem

Customer inquiries arriving through email can easily become difficult to track when they are mixed with other messages. Manually transferring information into a spreadsheet also creates repetitive work and increases the possibility of missing or inconsistently recording an inquiry.

The challenge was to design a workflow that could automatically identify relevant emails and move their information into a centralized tracking sheet.

How the Workflow Works

Step 01

Gmail Trigger

The workflow monitors Gmail for messages matching the configured search criteria.

Step 02

Zapier Automation

Zapier receives the matching email information and passes the relevant fields to the next step.

Step 03

Google Sheets

Email information is organized into a structured spreadsheet for easier tracking and review.

Information Captured

The tracking sheet was structured to store useful information from each matching message, including:

Date Sender Subject Email Body Email ID

Technologies Used

The project combines commonly used digital productivity platforms into a single automated workflow.
